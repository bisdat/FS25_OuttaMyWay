## TS004 — Attached-implement physical representation contrast

TS004 contains two AI-capable tractor–cultivator units used to challenge Condor-specific geometry assumptions:

- **unit1:** Valtra S 416 + Horsch Tiger 8 MT;
- **unit2:** John Deere 8RX 410 + Väderstad TopDown 600.

Prototype 12 already validated both combinations as coherent attached Physical Assemblies; the first exhibited Working-State Motion Divergence and the second sustained normal AI work.

Static source examination adds a deliberate internal-structure contrast:

- Tiger represents wing articulation through multiple physics components;
- TopDown represents folding collision-bearing descendants inside one physics component;
- direct collision-node mapping coverage differs;
- declared base size and working width provide state-scoped dimensional evidence rather than collision authority.

Use TS004 for future Member-Local Physical Resolution and representation-fallback experiments. Do not redistribute supplied GIANTS game assets in the repository; request them again when needed.

## TS008 — Condor agronomic-state contrast and dynamic extent

The 36 m Condor Endurance II initially encountered an incompatible harvest-ready state and stopped after brief admission (TS008-N). Changing wheat to `green big` and requiring fertiliser produced a positive native AI run through unfold, working, manoeuvring and completion (TS008-P). TS008-P satisfies Dynamic-Extent Positive; TS008-N strongly supports Execution-Time State Rejection but current observer sampling did not independently capture the brief lifecycle.

## TS009 — Olive crop-system admission rejection

## TS010 — Right-offset mower and work-envelope routing

Field 4 contained grass ready to cut. DEUTZ-FAHR 6135 C RVshift + SaMASZ XT 390 was admitted by native Giants AI. The mower remained offset to the tractor's right; Giants kept the mower at the field edge and used a spiral route through repeated working and manoeuvring. The Essential Evidence Horizon was reached before full completion. This satisfies Asymmetric Working Envelope and establishes Work-Envelope-Anchored Routing.
