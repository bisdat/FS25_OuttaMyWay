# Scenario Library

## TS001 — Head-on convergence and continuation

The existing TS001 game save is the observation fixture for Prototypes 01 through 04.

Two native GIANTS AI field workers follow independent routes that first produce harmless head-on proximity and later converge into a genuine conflict. The scenario must remain structurally unchanged so each prototype observes naturally occurring GIANTS behaviour rather than a path constructed around its thresholds.

- Prototype 01 established early conflict detectability.
- Prototype 02 distinguished transient conflict formation from established future conflict.
- Prototype 03 identified a temporal Candidate Option Preservation Window.
- Prototype 04 observes local intent expiry and whether a player-observed release remains safe through the Progress Entity's next repositioning event.

Prototype 04's limited procedure permits the player to stop and restart Patriot as a test stimulus. That action abandons and recreates GIANTS AI activity and is recorded as Job Restart Perturbation; it is not OuttaMyWay behaviour.

See `docs/prototypes/PROTOTYPE_04_CONTINUATION_INTENT.md` for the current hypothesis, evidence contract and test procedure.
