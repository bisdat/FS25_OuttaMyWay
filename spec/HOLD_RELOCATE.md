# Hold & Relocate Specification

## Identity and authority

**Specification Jurisdiction:** Hold & Relocate
**Jurisdiction ID:** `HOLD_RELOCATE`

**Primary Architecture Authority:** [Hold & Relocate](../architecture/BLOCKED_PROGRESS_QUALIFICATION.md#specification-jurisdiction--hold--relocate)

This contract operationalises the accepted **Hold & Relocate** responsibility in its governing Architecture. It is one cohesive responsibility, not a separate architectural jurisdiction per timing phase or source module. Native blocked Observation and Spatial Pair Inference provide candidates only; **current native commitment authority** must independently admit action: two current participants for a worker pair, or one native blocked field worker for solitary recovery. Neither Observation candidate authorises Control. GIANTS retains native blockage and productive route decisions.

## Single-worker extension — TEST 0.5.1.3, issue #461

A current active GIANTS FIELDWORK worker with positively observed native field-course `isBlocked` for **at least 1,000 ms**, but **without any eligible nearby second worker**, may be admitted independently as a **single-participant recovery commitment**. Admission re-reads that worker's current job, strategy, positive blocked state, and root pose and checks the local active worker registry; a synthetic second participant is forbidden. **No field-polygon membership or field-centroid proof is required for solitary recovery: native blockages outside field boundaries are ordinary cases.** Changed native job/strategy invalidates the commitment. Solo candidates and pair candidates are distinct current observation occurrences; a later genuine blocked occurrence has independent admission.

Once admitted, **the same existing Physical Control adapter and coordinator** must request TRANSIT without a fold-readiness wait, then perform GIANTS-native oblique reverse toward a **40 m relocation REGION**, preferring movement toward the centre of **the current worker's own GIANTS-generated field-course field**, but **never gating movement on field containment or destination eligibility**. 40 m is the **projected progress to the relocation region**, not an exact arrival waypoint, maximum travel watchdog, blocker width, or BWR steering look-ahead. The steering reference is subordinate and longer than the region, as for pairwise recovery. The independent directional identity comes from that worker's current `AIDriveStrategyFieldCourse.aiFieldCourse.fieldCourse.courseField.boundaryPositions`, yielding the native course polygon and its area centroid. Where a native course boundary is unavailable, use that same strategy's `fieldDetectionX/Z` as the inward reference if available. Only when both native sources are absent use the deterministic left-rear option. **An arbitrary nearby or adjacent field may never identify OUR field**; do not scan `g_fieldManager.fields` to select a solo destination or infer that a valid worker is invalid because its root is outside the polygon. Once measured region entry is confirmed, release reverse and immediately perform the same GIANTS FIELDWORK STOP/START replacement. No fictional blocker, blocker-width measurement, pairwise 1 km/h Regulation, pair release gate, or paired seven-second relocated-worker Hold is allowed in this four-stage solitary choreography.

## Commitment and evidence boundary

A **paired** Hold & Relocate coordination request MUST carry an independently issued commitment identity, two distinct current Physical Assembly references and current root positions, a field-polygon centroid, an explicit offset and an evidenced set of nearby blockers that includes the other pair participant. The independent commitment authority MUST positively validate the originating GIANTS Job Episode identity, its continuity and the freshness of the supplied physical evidence; these may be resolved from the authority's current state rather than trusted solely from request fields. The supplied centroid is the field's centroid, not the pair midpoint. The commitment validation mechanism must reject self-declared authority and stale, changed or unsupported native-job circumstances. Missing/contradictory evidence means no intervention; no default offset or invented blocker set.

The two pair participants remain unassigned as mover/protected assembly until after bounded TRANSIT preparation. For each mover assignment, the **other worker's** working width captured during FIELDWORK **+ 5 m** prescribes actual directional relocation progress; do not rank a shorter but insufficient footprint-edge movement ahead of the required distance. Select among routes which accommodate that full prescribed progress within the field; current centroid distance is only a deterministic preference when feasible alternatives are otherwise comparable. Runtime node keys are tie-breaking references, not durable semantic identity.

The immediate-vicinity blocker set and a safe physical reverse objective require upstream evidence; proximity is not independent causal proof. The coordinator consumes these inputs but **does not generate** their authority or discover them. No pair candidate from DEBUG publication directly creates Control.

**Admission Evidence Publication:** Every independently evaluated, qualifying native pair occurrence MUST produce either an admitted Control transition (and its outcome) or one explicit, reason-coded admission rejection at NORMAL publication level. A rejected candidate remains no-action; publication does **not** grant authority or justify weakening validation. Record whether the runtime listener is executing once per loaded map, so lack of a candidate handoff can be distinguished from a candidate which reached the authority but was rejected.
