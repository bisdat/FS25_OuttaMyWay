## Identity and authority

**Jurisdiction ID:** `HOLD_RELOCATE`

**Primary Architecture Authority:** [Hold & Relocate](../architecture/BLOCKED_PROGRESS_QUALIFICATION.md#specification-jurisdiction--hold--relocate)

This contract operationalises the accepted **Hold & Relocate** responsibility in its governing Architecture. Neither Observation candidate authorises Control. GIANTS retains native blockage and productive route decisions.

## Timed physical coordination

The governing Architecture defines the six-stage purpose and fixed policies; the implementation MUST preserve these consequences:

1. Begin **1 km/h maximum native-speed Regulation** of the other participant concurrently with the native TRANSIT request. The **5,000 ms** timer begins at Regulation acquisition, not after an idle phase. Preserve GIANTS native acceleration, steering, drive permission and travel direction.
2. **Start reverse immediately after an accepted TRANSIT request, within the same coordination operation.** Raise/fold commands may continue animating throughout relocation. There MUST NOT be a TRANSIT completion, fold-endpoint, lowering-state or readiness polling gate before reverse. A failed native request is still a failure requiring conservative cleanup; command acceptance does not imply physical TRANSIT completion.
3. Release the other participant's Regulation at **5,000 ms** by timer alone, not by pair clearance. Record the native drive interception count and root displacement while regulated. Report release failures for this collision without retaining previous job state.
4. Reverse using the GIANTS BWR-native reverser frame toward a signed **Cross-Track Egress Region**, with the nominal oblique vector length `blockerWorkingWidthM + 5 m`. Among both polygon-admissible candidates, choose the more field-inward/centroid-facing direction, irrespective of which side is already occupied; old-side status only breaks a tie. Completion is signed lateral projection against the blocker course normal, not longitudinal retreat, exact steering-reference arrival, or an arbitrary distance fail-safe. Native tool-relative steering adjustment and a separate look-ahead reference remain in force. The work-area width is a planning scale, not proof of collision clearance.
5. Only after positive **Cross-Track Region entry**, end reverse Control and Hold the relocated worker for **7,000 ms**. At timer expiry release that Hold without an additional pairwise clearance gate.
6. Immediately request a single synchronous native FIELDWORK stop/restart operation. The physical interface must report successful native stop and accepted new native start separately. A successful request does **not** prove productive continuation, which remains GIANTS Reality evidence.

The physical Control boundary must establish native TRANSIT requests, reverse steering and displacement observation, Hold acquire/release verification, lifecycle revalidation, and native stop/restart semantics. The coordinator may order those activities but MUST NOT be their authority source.

**Request-only TRANSIT:** a subordinate configuration mechanism consumes an upstream selected native request plan drawn from the Job Episode's capability evidence. It submits GIANTS work-off, raise and fold commands without polling native configuration endpoints or deriving a READY verdict. It reports only submission; reverse begins while configuration proceeds, including on assemblies that are not yet fully raised/folded. Failure after a partial native request must retain the obligation to issue restoration commands. A cancellation may request restoration without waiting for its completion; neither request nor cancellation proves physical posture. The absence of a readiness gate does not remove independent Pair Commitment, lifecycle checks or temporary native-control cleanup; it does not authorise an invented movement fail-safe. Physical safety during concurrent folding and reversing is an explicit unvalidated assumption for GIANTS Reality.

**Native FIELDWORK Hand-back** is a subordinate physical mechanism. Obtain the current independently admitted native FIELDWORK Job Episode and compare it once with the active job; identify its farm and prepare/validate a replacement before stopping the existing job. Then invoke `aiSystem:stopJob(originalJob, nil)` followed immediately by `aiSystem:startJob(replacement, farmId)`, with no intervening delay. A failed native call reports its immediate reason. The handback MUST NOT retain vehicle-keyed attempt history, maintain an uncertain native job state, repeat current-job checks during the stop/start transition, or impose post-start identity polling. No configuration-readiness or pair-clearance gate is added. GIANTS retains productive control; successful calls are not independent evidence of physical continuation.

**Translation-only Hold** is a subordinate physical mechanism: while expressly active for one vehicle, it suppresses propulsion permission and native requested speed at `AIVehicleUtil.driveToPoint()` while retaining the native steering target, travel direction and optional `doNotSteer` argument. It MUST NOT deny `getCanAIFieldWorkerContinueWork()` permission, because that native boundary also controls field-worker configuration progression. Unregulated workers must retain original native driving. A successful Hold call confirms only that the restriction has been armed, **not that vehicle motion has stopped**. Release removes that restriction without pair-clearance, native-blockage or separation tests. Loss of native job continuity relinquishes the old Hold rather than constraining the new GIANTS episode; checking merely that some job exists is insufficient.

## Implementation traceability and validation scope

The [coordinator](../scripts/coordination/HoldRelocateCoordinator.lua) currently realises role selection, coordination sequencing and conservative unresolved-effect retention. The [native reverse mechanism](../scripts/control/mechanisms/NativeReverseMechanism.lua) is a dormant subordinate: when explicitly commanded, it interposes on GIANTS driveToPoint for only the selected assembly, preserves unrelated calls (including doNotSteer), uses the GIANTS reverser frame and BWR tool-relative correction, and reports cumulative X/Z displacement. The [translation-only Hold mechanism](../scripts/control/mechanisms/NativeTranslationHoldMechanism.lua) independently wraps the same native drive entry point only while an explicitly commanded vehicle is Held, preserves field-worker continuation permission and releases the restriction on timer command or positive lifecycle invalidation. It records a GIANTS job-object **reference**, not a durable Job Episode identity; upstream commitment authority remains responsible for semantic episode continuity. The [native TRANSIT request mechanism](../scripts/control/mechanisms/NativeTransitRequestMechanism.lua) submits preselected work-off/raise/fold requests with no native readiness polling; its inverse requests on cancellation likewise make no settlement claim. The [native FIELDWORK job-replacement mechanism](../scripts/control/mechanisms/NativeFieldworkJobReplacementMechanism.lua) is a dormant subordinate based on the archived BWR native job API sequence; it prepares before stop, immediately starts after stop, verifies the replacement as the worker's current job where available, and retains any unverified post-stop outcome without automatic retry. The [live Pair Commitment authority](../scripts/coordination/NativePairCommitmentAuthority.lua) admits only independently current GIANTS pairs with common field-polygon geometry; [Physical Control](../scripts/control/HoldRelocatePhysicalControl.lua) binds the four native mechanisms behind the existing coordinator; and the [live runtime](../scripts/coordination/LiveHoldRelocateRuntime.lua) observes current pair nominations and enforces immediate disabled-state relinquishment. The shell instantiates this path on the server, subject to complete admission evidence. The new [live offline challenge](../tests/shell/live_hold_relocate.lua) exercises source identity, polygon centroid, selection, timed Hold and native-handoff sequencing under mocks. Local 8 km/h reverse speed and 1 m point-approach tolerance are implementation-owned calibration. A command or sampled displacement does not prove clearance, useful continuation or exact physical stopping. [Product entry](../scripts/main.lua) sources and constructs the live adapter, authority and coordinator. The [offline behaviour challenge](../tests/shell/hold_relocate.lua) covers timing, role selection, independent authority, failure recovery and uncertain native hand-back. The [reverse-mechanism offline challenge](../tests/shell/native_reverse_mechanism.lua) checks native call passthrough, reverser frame, available tool correction, measured travel, abort and missing APIs. The [translation-Hold challenge](../tests/shell/native_translation_hold_mechanism.lua) checks independent Hold targets, original native arguments, native permission separation, timer-independent release, positive player interlock, missing job and replacement of a non-null GIANTS job. Interleaving the two global native wrappers remains unvalidated against GIANTS Reality. The [TRANSIT request challenge](../tests/shell/native_transit_request_mechanism.lua) proves that requests do not poll readiness and that partial failures retain restoration access. The [FIELDWORK hand-back challenge](../tests/shell/native_fieldwork_job_replacement.lua) checks the native preparation-before-stop sequence, adjacent stop/start, independent source Job Episode identity, rejection before the commitment point, single-shot uncertainty and positive replacement evidence. GIANTS in-game tests, not offline mocks, must validate reverse geometry, physical stopping distance, Hold, concurrent configuration movement, native job recreation and productive continuation; TRANSIT readiness is intentionally **not** an action gate.
