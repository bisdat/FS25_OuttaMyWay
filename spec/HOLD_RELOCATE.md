# Hold & Relocate Specification

## Identity and authority

**Specification Jurisdiction:** Hold & Relocate
**Jurisdiction ID:** `HOLD_RELOCATE`

**Primary Architecture Authority:** [Hold & Relocate](../architecture/BLOCKED_PROGRESS_QUALIFICATION.md#specification-jurisdiction--hold--relocate)

This contract operationalises the accepted **Hold & Relocate** responsibility in its governing Architecture. It is one cohesive responsibility, not a separate architectural jurisdiction per timing phase or source module. Native blocked Observation and Spatial Pair Inference provide candidates only; **Pair Commitment** and Bounded Authority must independently admit action. GIANTS retains native blockage and productive route decisions.

## Commitment and evidence boundary

A Hold & Relocate coordination request MUST carry an independently issued commitment identity, two distinct current Physical Assembly references and current root positions, a field-polygon centroid, an explicit offset and an evidenced set of nearby blockers that includes the other pair participant. The independent commitment authority MUST positively validate the originating GIANTS Job Episode identity, its continuity and the freshness of the supplied physical evidence; these may be resolved from the authority's current state rather than trusted solely from request fields. The supplied centroid is the field's centroid, not the pair midpoint. The commitment validation mechanism must reject self-declared authority and stale, changed, unsupported or player-controlled circumstances. Missing/contradictory evidence means no intervention; no default offset or invented blocker set.

Of the paired assemblies, the relocator is the one whose root is **nearest to the field centroid** in horizontal X/Z distance, irrespective of which worker first reported blocked. An exact squared-distance tie is broken consistently using current assembly-reference keys; the tie-break does not confer enduring semantic identity on GIANTS node identifiers.

The immediate-vicinity blocker set and a safe physical reverse objective require upstream evidence; proximity is not independent causal proof. The coordinator consumes these inputs but **does not generate** their authority or discover them. No pair candidate from DEBUG publication directly creates Control.

**Admission Evidence Publication:** Every independently evaluated, qualifying native pair occurrence MUST produce either an admitted Control transition (and its outcome) or one explicit, reason-coded admission rejection at NORMAL publication level. A rejected candidate remains no-action; publication does **not** grant authority or justify weakening validation. Record whether the runtime listener is executing once per loaded map, so lack of a candidate handoff can be distinguished from a candidate which reached the authority but was rejected. A native pair occurrence is considered only once; a later occurrence can be considered independently. This diagnostic contract arises from the TEST 0.5.0.20 in-game observation of a 1,006 ms native candidate at 9.62 m with no corresponding resolution message. Its precise rejected predicate was **not observable in that build**. No assumption that FIELDWORK hand-back, TRANSIT, reverse or Hold ran is justified. TEST 0.5.0.21 adds publication of the distinct current-job, field-course, player-control, root-pose, and native field-geometry rejection reasons without changing admission policy.

**Player Control Interlock alignment (TEST 0.5.0.22):** a current player-control claim is an **independent positive veto**, not an obligation for every GIANTS vehicle to return a Boolean `false` from `getIsControlled()`. An explicit `true` from `getIsControlled()` or a match to `g_currentMission.controlledVehicle` forbids intervention; a thrown native call also rejects because the query failed. A missing method or a non-positive return is not positive player-control evidence and must be evaluated alongside the *separately mandatory* active native FIELDWORK Job Episode, native field-course strategy, server, identity, root geometry and Pair Commitment checks. This matches the pre-existing Translation Hold player-control witness contract, without allowing native-job or player-takeover authority to be inferred from an absent flag. The TEST 0.5.0.21 game log demonstrated a rejection coded `PLAYER_CONTROL_CLEARANCE_UNAVAILABLE`, but did not record the method's return or identify which assembly triggered it. No claim of physically successful relocation follows from the policy correction.

**Player-control witness provenance (TEST 0.5.0.23):** TEST 0.5.0.22 field observation **again** rejected a native-pair candidate (1,008 ms, 9.59 m) under `PLAYER_CONTROL_CLEARANCE_UNAVAILABLE`, despite removal of the strict explicit-false requirement. This disproves the sufficiency of the .22 hypothesis. Because the log did not identify which participant or which positive/failing GIANTS control surface caused refusal, the next build preserves the positive-player-takeover veto unchanged and publishes the rejected participant (FIRST/SECOND), its root identity, the specific `getIsControlled`/mission witness, and the independent `getIsEntered` reading. Entry is **diagnostic corroboration only**, not permission to override a positive claim. A native getter exception still rejects; absent and non-positive returns still do not alone reject. Do not relax the interlock or infer legitimate autonomous control until new GIANTS Reality provides discriminating evidence.

**Current live admission:** a separate native Pair Commitment authority reads a native candidate occurrence, then independently verifies the ≥1 s observed pulse, both current GIANTS FIELDWORK worker/job/strategy references, player-control state, ≤30 m current root locality and membership in the **same GIANTS density-map field polygon**. It derives the polygon's area centroid from native world X/Z points; missing, degenerate or contradictory geometry produces no Control. Only the original pair partner is currently admitted to the immediate blocker set; other neighbouring obstacles are outside this first live validation envelope. This is not a new collision detector or proof of causal obstruction. The authority issues a commitment reference that alone can be validated by the coordinator. On the first live tranche the explicitly selected offset is **0 m** (maximum reverse extent 30 m); a positive extra offset is not inferred or invented. This is a deliberately narrower implementation choice, not a revised architectural maximum.

## Timed physical coordination

The governing Architecture defines the six-stage purpose and fixed policies; the implementation MUST preserve these consequences:

1. Begin egress protection for all validated immediate blockers concurrently with the native TRANSIT request. The **5,000 ms** Hold begins when egress protection is acquired, not after an invented idle phase.
2. **Start reverse immediately after an accepted TRANSIT request, within the same coordination operation.** Raise/fold commands may continue animating throughout relocation. There MUST NOT be a TRANSIT completion, fold-endpoint, lowering-state or readiness polling gate before reverse. A failed native request is still a failure requiring conservative cleanup; command acceptance does not imply physical TRANSIT completion.
3. Release the blocker Hold on expiry of the **5,000 ms** timer alone, not on pair distance, blocker unblocking or geometric clearance. If physical release cannot be verified, retain the unresolved obligation and request intervention; the controller must not claim release.
4. Reverse toward the field centroid, limiting physical travel to **30 m + the explicitly provided offset**. The former BWR **40 m steering horizon** is a local steering reference, not travel permission. Native reverse requires `getAIReverserNode()`, tool-relative target adjustment where available, and local-space `AIVehicleUtil.driveToPoint(..., moveForwards=false, ...)`. A reverse flag on a forward target is insufficient.
5. Only after positive bounded movement completion, end reverse Control and Hold the relocated worker for **10,000 ms**. At timer expiry release that Hold without an additional pairwise clearance gate.
6. Immediately request a single synchronous native FIELDWORK stop/restart operation. The physical interface must report successful native stop and accepted new native start separately. A successful request does **not** prove productive continuation, which remains GIANTS Reality evidence.

The physical Control boundary must establish native TRANSIT requests, reverse steering and displacement observation, Hold acquire/release verification, lifecycle revalidation, and native stop/restart semantics. The coordinator may order those activities but MUST NOT be their authority source.

**Request-only TRANSIT:** a subordinate configuration mechanism consumes an upstream selected native request plan drawn from the Job Episode's capability evidence. It submits GIANTS work-off, raise and fold commands without polling native configuration endpoints or deriving a READY verdict. It reports only submission; reverse begins while configuration proceeds, including on assemblies that are not yet fully raised/folded. Failure after a partial native request must retain the obligation to issue restoration commands. A cancellation may request restoration without waiting for its completion; neither request nor cancellation proves physical posture. The absence of a readiness gate does not remove independent Pair Commitment, lifecycle checks, movement bounds or fail-safe handling. Physical safety during concurrent folding and reversing is an explicit unvalidated assumption for GIANTS Reality.

**Native FIELDWORK Hand-back** is a subordinate physical mechanism, not a second admission authority. Immediately before replacing the job it MUST obtain the independently admitted originating GIANTS Job Episode's current job reference, reject a different current job, verify the native FIELDWORK type, obtain the active AI job farm, and prepare/validate a new native FIELDWORK job **before any stop**. It MUST then invoke native `aiSystem:stopJob(originalJob, nil)` followed directly by `aiSystem:startJob(replacement, farmId)` in one synchronous execution step. It MUST NOT add a fold-settlement, pair-clearance, route-continuation or timed-delay gate. Once the native stop invocation has begun, even a thrown exception or missing positive post-start evidence is an **unresolved native hand-back**, not a reason to repeat the stop/start cycle. **After that commitment point, pre-restart TRANSIT restoration commands MUST NOT be replayed onto the vehicle while native job identity is uncertain.** Retain that restoration obligation as unresolved for player intervention rather than modifying a possible successor Job Episode. The mechanism may report `isOldJobStopped=true` and `isNewJobStarted=true` only with corroborating native current-job replacement evidence, not merely because `pcall` returned. Positive hand-back evidence establishes native job acceptance, **not actual productivity**. The live adapter/Job Episode source and GIANTS behaviour remain unvalidated.

**Translation-only Hold** is a subordinate physical mechanism: while expressly active for one vehicle, it suppresses propulsion permission and native requested speed at `AIVehicleUtil.driveToPoint()` while retaining the native steering target, travel direction and optional `doNotSteer` argument. It MUST NOT deny `getCanAIFieldWorkerContinueWork()` permission, because that native boundary also controls field-worker configuration progression. Non-Held workers must retain original native driving. A successful Hold call confirms only that the restriction has been armed, **not that vehicle motion has stopped**. Release removes that restriction without pair-clearance, native-blockage or separation tests. Loss of native job continuity or positive player takeover relinquishes the old Hold rather than constraining the new GIANTS episode; checking merely that some job exists is insufficient.

## Failure and unresolved obligations

Unavailability of a commitment, source GIANTS Job Episode, player consent, clock or native API must fail closed. On actuation failure, disablement or episode/strategy turnover, neutralise commanded movement and release Hold/TRANSIT using the available physical interface.

An external call can fail **after** partly changing GIANTS state. The coordinator MUST track possibly acquired effects before invoking it and must not discard a failed cleanup. Unverified release or job replacement is `UNRESOLVED`, with retained evidence and a supported explicit cleanup retry / player-intervention route. An uncertain native restart is not recast as GIANTS successful continuation even when physical Hold cleanup succeeds.

**Current implementation boundary:** the shell now constructs a live server-gated native Pair Commitment authority, bounded Physical Control adapter and Hold & Relocate runtime. The existing four native mechanisms are instantiated by that adapter and may command eligible GIANTS workers. This is an **executable in-game test candidate, not an in-game-validated physical capability**. Native candidate Observation remains read-only and merely exposes live pair evidence; the separate authority controls admission. Incomplete player/job/strategy, field geometry, assembly capability or configuration evidence denies intervention. The first live path captures a native configuration plan at admission from attached assembly members; a durable Job Episode bootstrap cache and multi-party obstacle membership remain unimplemented. Runtime status publication is diagnostic evidence, not a complete player-facing waiting/HUD contract. Representative FS25 reality validation remains mandatory for actual movement, combined wrappers, concurrent folding/reversing, Hold release, disablement and native job hand-back.

## Implementation traceability and validation scope

The [coordinator](../scripts/coordination/HoldRelocateCoordinator.lua) currently realises role selection, coordination sequencing and conservative unresolved-effect retention. The [native reverse mechanism](../scripts/control/mechanisms/NativeReverseMechanism.lua) is a dormant subordinate: when explicitly commanded, it interposes on GIANTS driveToPoint for only the selected assembly, preserves unrelated calls (including doNotSteer), uses the GIANTS reverser frame and BWR tool-relative correction, and reports cumulative X/Z displacement. The [translation-only Hold mechanism](../scripts/control/mechanisms/NativeTranslationHoldMechanism.lua) independently wraps the same native drive entry point only while an explicitly commanded vehicle is Held, preserves field-worker continuation permission and releases the restriction on timer command or positive lifecycle invalidation. It records a GIANTS job-object **reference**, not a durable Job Episode identity; upstream commitment authority remains responsible for semantic episode continuity. The [native TRANSIT request mechanism](../scripts/control/mechanisms/NativeTransitRequestMechanism.lua) submits preselected work-off/raise/fold requests with no native readiness polling; its inverse requests on cancellation likewise make no settlement claim. The [native FIELDWORK job-replacement mechanism](../scripts/control/mechanisms/NativeFieldworkJobReplacementMechanism.lua) is a dormant subordinate based on the archived BWR native job API sequence; it prepares before stop, immediately starts after stop, verifies the replacement as the worker's current job where available, and retains any unverified post-stop outcome without automatic retry. The [live Pair Commitment authority](../scripts/coordination/NativePairCommitmentAuthority.lua) admits only independently current GIANTS pairs with common field-polygon geometry; [Physical Control](../scripts/control/HoldRelocatePhysicalControl.lua) binds the four native mechanisms behind the existing coordinator; and the [live runtime](../scripts/coordination/LiveHoldRelocateRuntime.lua) observes current pair nominations and enforces immediate disabled-state relinquishment. The shell instantiates this path on the server, subject to complete admission evidence. The new [live offline challenge](../tests/shell/live_hold_relocate.lua) exercises source identity, polygon centroid, selection, timed Hold and native-handoff sequencing under mocks. Local 8 km/h reverse speed and 1 m point-approach tolerance are implementation-owned calibration. A command or sampled displacement does not prove clearance, useful continuation or exact physical stopping. [Product entry](../scripts/main.lua) sources and constructs the live adapter, authority and coordinator. The [offline behaviour challenge](../tests/shell/hold_relocate.lua) covers timing, role selection, independent authority, failure recovery and uncertain native hand-back. The [reverse-mechanism offline challenge](../tests/shell/native_reverse_mechanism.lua) checks native call passthrough, reverser frame, available tool correction, measured travel, abort and missing APIs. The [translation-Hold challenge](../tests/shell/native_translation_hold_mechanism.lua) checks independent Hold targets, original native arguments, native permission separation, timer-independent release, positive player interlock, missing job and replacement of a non-null GIANTS job. Interleaving the two global native wrappers remains unvalidated against GIANTS Reality. The [TRANSIT request challenge](../tests/shell/native_transit_request_mechanism.lua) proves that requests do not poll readiness and that partial failures retain restoration access. The [FIELDWORK hand-back challenge](../tests/shell/native_fieldwork_job_replacement.lua) checks the native preparation-before-stop sequence, adjacent stop/start, independent source Job Episode identity, rejection before the commitment point, single-shot uncertainty and positive replacement evidence. GIANTS in-game tests, not offline mocks, must validate reverse geometry, physical stopping distance, Hold, concurrent configuration movement, native job recreation and productive continuation; TRANSIT readiness is intentionally **not** an action gate.
