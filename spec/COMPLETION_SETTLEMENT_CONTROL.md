# Completion Settlement Control — Spatially Selected Completion Parking

## Identity and authority

**Specification Jurisdiction:** Completion Settlement Control  
**Jurisdiction ID:** `COMPLETION_SETTLEMENT_CONTROL`

**Primary Architecture Authority:** [Completion-Triggered Terminal Egress — Completion Settlement Control](../architecture/COMPLETION_TRIGGERED_TERMINAL_EGRESS.md#specification-jurisdiction--completion-settlement-control)

**Current implementation:** Parking retains a unique registered Source Field observed during the exact active FIELDWORK Job Episode (or from uniquely valid completion-time root membership), evaluates candidate edges and supports captured Physical Assemblies with one or more selected members. GIANTS-native forward and reverse actuation is reused; nominal planned member envelopes are checked against *current* real member poses during movement and again at the stopped terminal. Field 77 TS019 is the successful physical regression; TS004 exposed and motivated removal of the obsolete categorical multi-member veto. Actual articulated parking on TS004 and the separately unresolved incomplete-guide Safety Stop remain unvalidated.

## Admission

A real native successful GIANTS `AIJobFieldWork` stop is the only trigger. The independent [Completion Transit](COMPLETION_TRANSIT.md) consumer receives completion first. Parking receives a separate subsequent notification; it does not inspect TRANSIT acceptance, folding completion or transport readback. Its own Configuration, physical evidence and Control admission govern parking. A manual job stop, new AI job, disabled mod, missing native vehicle identity, an active blocked-recovery intervention, missing Source Field identity, unqualified current pose/geometry or an unsafe corridor vetoes Control.

**Current admission:** Physical Completion Parking is On by default only after master player Configuration `enabled=true` is durably resolved and the server, positive native FIELDWORK completion, uniquely observed registered Source Field, complete-member geometry and existing ownership/safety conditions admit Control. It no longer reads `diagnostics.xml`; publication settings cannot enable/disable vehicle movement. Master Enabled off still supersedes pending/active parking. A dedicated player `Full Completion Parking` option, **default On**, will be implemented later under reopened [#139](https://github.com/bisdat/FS25_OuttaMyWay/issues/139), separate from Completion TRANSIT request-only. The option is **not yet available in General Settings**.

The original Field 77/edge 9 gate was a temporary experimental fixture; it is not a supported admission rule. Historical observations remain in [Completion Parking Research](../docs/research/COMPLETION_SETTLEMENT_PROTOTYPE_TEST.md). No field identifier, registered edge index or attached-member count may substitute for actual geometry qualification.

## Fresh Assembly Field Occupancy and disposition

Following a successful native FIELDWORK completion, independently of the earlier Completion TRANSIT request outcome, take the current selected-member nominal base-size rectangles from `PairTransitRegion.capture()` and compare each rectangle to the retained Source Field polygon using `SettlementCorridorAssessment.shapeIntersectsPolygon()`. No extra boom-extension or fold-readiness inspection is part of this classification. A root outside does not imply that an attached implement has cleared the field. Classify **INSIDE**, **STRADDLING**, **CLEAR** or unavailable evidence (no false outside result). All selected members clear of the Source Field means **COMPLETION_SETTLEMENT_NOT_REQUIRED**: log the no-action outcome without motor Control, terminal claim or fabricated parked-terminal verification. Reassess when the assembly reaches the head of the completion queue.

For INSIDE, first try the local actual-pose route; after that fails, an allocator may compare candidate terminals only if the real current-pose extended approach to each candidate entry also succeeds. For STRADDLING, try local routes from the actual pose across eligible edges/stations, not a hypothetical interior entry. The existing Extended Infield Approach remains restricted to its supported interior start; an exterior-to-infield re-entry is not silently authorised. A candidate becomes claimable **only when a complete current-pose-connected movement plan and local terminal manoeuvre are both positively qualified**. Native TRANSIT selection, command dispatch and Control stopping rules remain separate and unchanged.

## General Source Field and eligible parking edges

The native successful FIELDWORK stop triggers the candidate. During active FIELDWORK, a unique registered-polygon root membership is retained for the exact native job and assembly. A bounded once-per-second active registry census performs at most one new field-registry scan per tick, rotating among unresolved jobs. A positive witness stops that job's field scanning; ambiguous or outside samples may be retried. The final worker leaving the active registry does not erase positive evidence. The native successful stop consumes the witness; unsuccessful stop discards it. A new job requires fresh spatial evidence, even on the same assembly. If no work-time witness was established, completion-time unique registered root containment is a conservative fallback; never select a nearest field, displayed farmland label or unverified native course/detection seed. A shared geometry context discovers registered polygon edges whose outward normal is positively resolved and whose nominal length exceeds the six metres needed for the terminal station's minimum three-metre endpoint margins. That is only a permissive candidate filter; actual member fit is decided later. A local parking plan first tries the nearest geometrically eligible edge. If that yields no feasible nominal local route, incremental Parking Region Allocation compares distributed entry stations across the *full usable length* of every eligible edge, including observed working-assembly Situation Assessment and full physical-occupancy exclusion. A historic eight-station-at-the-first-195-metres pattern and 64-total-site cutoff are not admission rules. A candidate terminal can only be claimed after its own local manoeuvre is qualified. The selected field and edge are retained through extended infield approach, stopped handoff, replanning and final whole-nominal-assembly terminal verification. Source Field identity is sticky for this completion responsibility; root movement beyond the field boundary does not relabel the episode. A local plan may assess an exterior starting root only within its selected edge's existing bounded band, with the usual full-member geometry, neighbour and physical occupancy screening. The extended infield lattice still requires an infield start; a safe exterior-to-interior return requires a separately qualified route and cannot be inferred from field identity. No field number or edge index grants or vetoes parking authority. Geometry and nominal physical occupancy are not road/ditch/hedge or tyre-swept safety guarantees; multi-field GIANTS execution remains the validator.

## Governing terminal objective

**Architecture authority:** [Pose-Conditioned Terminal Settlement](../architecture/COMPLETION_TRIGGERED_TERMINAL_EGRESS.md#pose-conditioned-terminal-settlement).

The subsequent architectural decision defines an **entire-assembly terminal parking region between field boundaries** as the objective, with **success anywhere along a suitable edge**, not a specific point or mandated full-edge traverse. It prefers **brief reverse followed by forward entry** when feasible, otherwise a pose-conditioned manoeuvre such as forward J-entry. Reassess from each independently completed worker's actual root/attached-member pose. The necessary forward distance depends on assembly clearance and final stopping feasibility, not a predetermined 60 m or ~200 m route literal.

**TEST `0.5.5.8` behaviour below is historical implementation, now known not to realise this goal.** In particular its maximum-sweep candidate selection, fixed far staging point, three stop-and-release waypoint transitions, forward-only direction and endpoint-minus-3 m terminal target are prototype-specific and **not accepted architectural constraints**. Do not silently alter or portray those clauses as a future general product contract. The next Control contract must be decided and separately authorised after assessing progressive forward/reverse guidance, physical swept envelopes, continuous waypoint tracking, suitable terminal region membership, stopping, and interruption.

The existing player-Control veto arose in `0.5.5.8` without a completed architectural decision; the agreed direction was to **retain it temporarily without retroactive architectural acceptance**. The consent gate remains separately required; no TEST executable change is implied by this specification annotation.

## Route qualification and native physical action

Candidate local manoeuvres comprise bounded short reverse/forward curves and forward heading acquisition from the *actual* post-job assembly pose; a distant candidate may need a separate qualified extended infield approach. The planner captures current member rectangles and uses them to shortlist route candidates against source, neighbouring registered fields, selected edge projections and current physical occupancy. These are **nominal predicted sweeps**, not a claim to forecast every hitch angle, wheel track, obstacle or collision shape. The historical single-member-only admission was an expedient for early sprayer experiments and is retired.

During actual native forward/reverse movement, the established `NativeStaticAssemblyDriveMechanism` uses the appropriate GIANTS steering/reverser frames; it must not acquire an independent driving implementation for articulated equipment. **Straight reverse steering look-through is distinct from reverse waypoint arrival**: the initial straight retreat uses a BWR-evidenced long steering reference beyond its last reverse point (40 m), fed through the same GIANTS tool reverser correction and vehicle reverse-local transform. All short reverse waypoints, root-progress acceptance, movement bounds, live route guards, stop/cruise-OFF and per-episode Control authority remain unchanged by that look-through. This is an applied hypothesis for Parking, not a GIANTS-proven safe articulated steering result. Curved forward guidance does not inherit it. The guidance guards recapture current selected-member rectangles, refuse changed assembly identity, neighbour-field occupation or member movement beyond the nominated exit corridor; Extended Infield Approach likewise rechecks live member containment. A materially different realised articulated pose is evidence, not automatically equivalent to a rigidly rotated snapshot. Trajectory divergence invalidates the current planned route and triggers bounded reassessment: neutralise the active native drive, confirm a stable cruise-OFF stop, release the lease, then qualify an alternative from the complete assembly's actual current pose before any new movement. Divergence does not justify either automatic abandonment or blindly repeating the same route. Only an admissible, independently stopped complete assembly may receive terminal success.

The incremental planner budgets geometry per update and maintains finite Control and leg deadlines. These are bounded-work/supersession safeguards, not unsupported equipment or map-identity exclusions. Route refusal must describe the failed spatial, physical, native or timing contract; a failed candidate is not permission to move blindly.
**Planning Responsiveness (TEST calibration):** Pending Completion Parking assessment has its own **20,000 ms cumulative decision-response budget**, independent of the existing 240,000 ms overall physical Control/episode safety lease. This is an initial GIANTS test hypothesis, not a measured player-acceptance SLA. The clock spans first local qualification and fallback allocation without resetting on transition; a newly stopped and physically released worker may begin a distinct bounded planning phase from its *new actual pose*. Once the pending budget expires, OMW must refuse parking explicitly as `PARKING_PLANNING_RESPONSE_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED` without claiming or moving into an unqualified space. No expiration certifies clearance.

Allocation-only local route search may return at its **first fully qualified** approach/terminal/run-out candidate rather than optimize all nominal variants at a site; the ordinary initial nearest-edge route search and all physical admission, occupancy, claim, Stop and terminal verification rules remain unchanged. Frame workload remains capped at 320 weighted geometry units per update. When DIAGNOSTIC logging is eligible, record at most one bounded fallback-allocation progress snapshot around five seconds, including current site and cumulative work, so an early-ended GIANTS capture is interpretable without repeated log publication. General Operational Player Message visibility/lifecycle is independently governed by issue #89, not by diagnostic logging or this computational limit.

**Terminal Placement Reassessment:** When the final native guidance is finished, the drive has been released only after independently verified cruise-OFF physical stillness. A refused whole-assembly terminal must identify the failed geometric predicate; it is **not** success merely because the root stopped within the 2.5 m guidance tolerance. One bounded forward-only current-pose correction may be admitted after fresh complete-member, source/adjacent-field polygon, other-vehicle occupancy and retained terminal-claim qualification. A straight continuation of up to 8 m is tried first where the existing heading already permits it. When terminal heading is refused, **Forward Alignment Correction** may instead sample a short forward circular arc toward either nominated edge tangent followed by straight approach, up to 12 m combined travel. The entire nominal transition is sampled at approximately 1 m, with finite turning-radius hypotheses (12, 18 or 24 m) and no reverse phase. Candidate approach, terminal, early arrival (0.75 m short) and an additional 5 m stopping reserve must qualify. The heading-changing route uses tighter 0.75 m waypoint-arrival acceptance, but physical Control and independent actual-pose terminal verification still decide success. This is a testable native steering hypothesis, not a proof that GIANTS follows the sampled curve, nor static-pole, road or ditch clearance. The correction executes through an entirely new native drive lease and still requires a second real stopped whole-assembly verification. Otherwise (or after a failed correction), the already bounded stopped-alternative planner may reassess at most once, **re-observing the actual current complete assembly's INSIDE / STRADDLING occupancy** before constructing any candidate; no imaginary INSIDE entry is substituted for a STRADDLING assembly. Unknown geometry refuses movement. This remains nominal field geometry, **not proof of static-pole, road or ditch clearance**, and its physical safety and effectiveness require GIANTS Reality. An invalid terminal is never silently accepted or patched by unconditional extra travel.

**Read-only candidate-edge comparison:** If DIAGNOSTIC publication is enabled at a started Completion Parking episode, a separate bounded exterior-margin observation compares the geometrically nominated Source Field edges. It publishes one `COMPLETION_SETTLEMENT_MARGIN_COMPARISON` line reporting the nearest-root edge, each edge's length/root distance, sampled stationary nominal full-assembly polygon fit and optional midpoint terrain elevation difference. Road, poles, ditches, terrain passability, continuous approach/run-out and safe final parking are explicitly unassessed/unknown. The snapshot does not rank/select a different destination, claim a terminal or affect physical Control. No comparison work runs with diagnostics disabled.

**Failed Parking Region Allocation evidence:** When DIAGNOSTIC publication is eligible, the planner retains a bounded per-session count of candidate stations examined, first site-prefilter refusals, failed local route attempts, and the local planner's already-named geometric rejection predicates. On allocation failure, the runtime publishes one aggregate diagnostic line with the Source Field, assembly occupancy classification, and rejection distribution. Collection and aggregation are inactive when DIAGNOSTIC is suppressed. This evidence does not alter candidate eligibility, grant Control or make a terminal claim. In particular, a final `NO_QUALIFIED_TERMINAL_REGION` alone is not an explanation of which predicate made the candidate set empty. Each station's Situation Assessment must use the retained Source Field polygon from that allocation session.

## Terminal and interruption

After the final forward leg stops, recapture the *current* attached assembly nominal member geometry. A successful TEST observation requires unchanged member count, the root and **every captured member rectangle disjoint from the source and other registered field polygons**. If any member remains inside or current membership cannot be observed, report an interrupted or incomplete terminal rather than celebrating root arrival.

Root arrival at a waypoint is **not** physical stopping. After each leg's arrival, retain the native actuator lease and issue `driveToPoint` with driving disabled, steering neutral and native cruise OFF **every update**. Continue until `lastSpeedReal` is at most 0.12 m/s, and root displacement stays within 0.20 m, for at least 1,200 ms of continuous measured stillness. Require positive cruise-OFF observation before releasing the leg. If speed/position/confirmation is unavailable, Control interrupts without declaring successful settlement. This contract is independently testable from polygon terminal occupancy.

Releasing a Completion Settlement leg keeps native cruise OFF, restores the prior forward/reverse cruise *limits* only and requests the engine to stop where no newer native AI job or player-Control claim has taken over. The prior ACTIVE cruise state must **not** be reactivated on a parked completed vehicle. A final `COMPLETION_SETTLEMENT_FINISHED` requires both verified stopped final-leg release and the fresh full-member geometry check. Disable, a new native AI job or a positive player-Control selection must revoke physical movement and request bounded neutralisation; engine control must not be stolen from a newly active job or player-controlled vehicle. Map teardown discards deleted physical references without issuing stale engine calls. Any failure to neutralise is published as a warning.

**Player Control interlock:** at admission and throughout execution, a positive `getIsControlled()` or `g_currentMission.controlledVehicle` root selection preempts Completion Settlement. A missing or false witness cannot establish exclusive ownership; it only does not veto this tightly opted-in TEST. No active player steering and OuttaMyWay steering may be combined to satisfy a successful autonomous motion test. The turn path remains the same three waypoint legs as TEST 0.5.5.7; a J-entry trajectory change is deliberately deferred until independent steering is retested.

There is no generally verified dynamic-worker demand arbitration, collision-complete physical safety proof, or universal off-field parking allocation. Any such unsupported assumption must remain falsifiable during the explicitly opted-in prototype.

They must not be computed when DIAGNOSTIC is disabled.

## TEST 0.5.5.9 — pose-conditioned regional guidance (historical experiment)

This revises the **historical 0.5.5.8 three-leg route**, which remains described above as evidence of the rejected approach. Only the explicit prototype on registered Field 77 native edge 9 is changed. The owner-confirmed objective is **any stationary complete-assembly pose in a suitable inter-field parking strip** rather than a far longitudinal waypoint. Route planning captures the *current completed vehicle pose and nominal attached-member rectangles* and assesses both directions along the preferred edge.

**Entry candidate generation (bounded TEST hypotheses):** nominal rigid-member paths from the current heading use a short reverse (trial lengths 0, 12, 18, 24 or 30 metres), followed by two opposing forward arcs to move outward while returning to an edge-parallel heading; alternatively, a forward heading-acquisition J turn precedes the outward S-entry. Test radii 12/18/24 m and heading excursion samples 35/45/55/65/75°. Check each ~5-m guidance sample against known neighbouring registered fields, source-field containment during reverse, edge projection and the configured exterior band. *Neither sampled kinematics nor nominal rectangles establish GIANTS tire/implement swept clearance or road/ditch physics.* A candidate exists only if a complete-member terminal region witness is reached within a short continuation. Prefer lower total movement distance over a longer nominal parking strip. A second worker at a different completion pose is replanned independently and may receive different guidance and a different stop station. Refuse with `NO_POSE_REACHABLE_PARKING_REGION` when no sampled candidate exists rather than driving toward a point behind the vehicle.

**Steering contract:** the short targets are **continuous guidance**, not mandatory individual stops. With unchanged travel direction, retarget the native steering point under the same leased movement Control. Reverse→forward or terminal parking transitions require a positive physical stop, cruise OFF, and separate native re-arming. Native reverse uses GIANTS' reverser node and the existing native tool-relative reverse transform. The implicit 8 km/h experimental limit is **not restored**: a per-target braking-envelope speed `sqrt(2 × 1.5 m/s² × clamp(next-target distance, 2.5–12m))` (converted to km/h) is a tunable, unvalidated experimental model constrained by each direction's native motor/cruise ceiling. In particular, this is **not a claim of proven achievable deceleration** or permission to travel at full motor speed into a short stop corridor.

**Observed safety contracts:** an active guidance vector may not accumulate more than 5 m negative signed progress, the root must remain in the source field or the bounded nominated exterior edge band, and all native Control still obeys 240s total, 180s segment, disable/job supersession and the **retained owner-instructed** player-Control interlock. A final waypoint arrival alone cannot assert success; only physical stopping followed by recaptured current complete-member rectangles, outside the source and neighbouring registered field polygons and within the nominal edge strip, may produce `COMPLETION_SETTLEMENT_FINISHED`. The whole-member physical envelope is still incomplete in the presence of changing articulation or other world obstacles. The first field test must use the single-member TS018 sprayer.

**Required validation:** old `0.5.5.8` near-straight drive must not recur for an unreachable distant point; observe short target progression, any reverse→forward stop, actual steering curvature, whole-member final geometry and zero speed *after* completion. Assert CI BWR non-regression, pose-dependent varied terminal station, route refusal when infeasible and continuous same-direction retarget. No field success is inferred from CI. The route is *not* authorised for general maps or as universal reverse capability.

**Retired scope limitation:** TEST `0.5.5.9` formerly refused multi-member Physical Assemblies with `ARTICULATED_ENTRY_REALITY_UNVALIDATED`. This was a temporary sprayer-only test restriction, **not** an architectural admission rule. TEST `0.5.5.30` removes the blanket veto and reobserves actual member positions during movement. Its real-world behaviour remains subject to TS004 validation.

## TEST 0.5.5.10 — corrections after TS018 .9 FAIL

The TS018 test of 0.5.5.9 established two separate defects: a **10.974 s synchronous candidate-selection stall** following native completion and the immediate **SETTLEMENT_NEUTRAL_REQUEST_UNCONFIRMED** at reverse→forward handover. This revision corrects only those defects and the already-discussed approach braking; the Field 77 edge 9 nominal candidate families and single-member limitation remain in force.

**Frame-Budgeted Planning (before Control admission):** successful fieldwork completion only creates an inexpensive coroutine planning session and publishes `COMPLETION_SETTLEMENT_PLANNING_STARTED`. The session is resumed at most once per ordinary game update with **320 weighted geometry-work units**. Each neighbour AABB check consumes one unit, each potentially expensive exact polygon intersection an additional 24; the native completion callback never executes route enumeration. A position candidate can become physical Control only after the planning session reports `DONE`; pending or failed planning does not issue any motor/cruise/steering command. Route choice must remain identical to the reference sequential planner for the same completion pose. A planned session may be abandoned due to disable, native new-job supersession, the **unchanged 0.5.5.8 player-Control interlock**, map teardown or finite existing total lease expiry. This is cooperative main-thread work scheduling, not parallel engine access. Published admission reports weighted checks and resume count.

**Native Motor Request Versus Physical Motion:** Request native motor start when not positively observed already running. A false, absent or unreadable `getIsMotorStarted()` value is **not an admission condition** and must not suppress `AIVehicleUtil.driveToPoint` after the start request. A native start call that actually errors or expressly refuses is a command-dispatch failure, distinct from asynchronous motor-running readback. Actual route progress, AI/player Control supersession, live route/occupancy guards and the existing sustained physical stop remain authoritative. An accepted request or issued steering command does not establish movement or terminal success. This rule applies through the shared native static-drive mechanism; it creates no new TRANSIT or folding gate.

**Neutral / cruising state ordering:** during STOPPING, issue GIANTS `driveToPoint` with zero acceleration and driving disabled, **then** call `setCruiseControlState(OFF)`, **then** read and verify the cruise state. Repeat every update while speed is nonzero. GIANTS' native command may itself activate cruise, so ordering is material. Continue to require actual measured native speed ≤0.12 m/s plus ≤0.2 m root displacement during ≥1.2 s continuous stillness before releasing or rearming in the opposite direction.

**Pre-transition braking:** for a direction-change or final stop, speed demand is recalculated during motion from the remaining distance through all same-direction guidance points until the stop. The existing tentative deceleration 1.5 m/s² and 2.5 m arrival region shape the commanded speed while respecting native motor/cruise limits; a small 2.5 km/h minimum speed avoids demanding motion at zero cruise before the end region. This is a falsifiable engine-dynamics hypothesis, not proof that stopping distance is adequate. No fixed global 8 km/h cap is restored.

**GIANTS validation obligation:** measure the actual time between fieldwork completion and route admission, confirm no HUD/world freeze, observe reverse approach speed declining before direction-change STOPPING, confirm cruise OFF *after* the neutral native call, and witness stable physical stop then forward re-arming. A zero-speed log alone is insufficient if the vehicle subsequently accelerates. Do not promote on CI alone.

## TEST 0.5.5.11 — Coroutine-independent incremental assessment

**Owner-authorised correction:** the preceding 0.5.5.10 test refused admission with `COROUTINE_UNAVAILABLE` at the same instant as native AI completion. No planning or driving happened, and absence of a soft-lock did not validate scheduling. TEST 0.5.5.11 replaces **only the implementation of `CompletionSettlementRoute.beginPlan / advancePlan`** with an explicit Lua planning state machine. The 0.5.5.10 waypoint planner (`Route.plan`) is preserved as a pure synchronous **offline reference**, but **never called from the live completion event or update path**. No coroutine capability is required.

**Continuation state:** current completion pose, registered field polygon and neighbours, candidate loop indices (edge direction, reverse distance, trial radius, turn angle, method), current manoeuvre stage, partial track and guidance-segment index, current nominal member geometry and neighbour polygon index, and the best complete candidate. The scheduler suspends **mid-candidate and mid-member-neighbour assessment**, not just between entire routes. One `advancePlan` call from each update receives the existing allowance of **320 weighted work units**, charging each neighbour AABB comparison 1 and each full polygon intersection 24. Route candidates are admitted only on final `DONE`, after all candidate evaluations. A partial candidate is not a driveable plan. Unchanged finite control-deadline, cancellation, player-Control veto and accepted completion TRANSIT semantics apply.

**Parity requirement:** for the same static completion pose and Field 77 registered polygons, both the incremental and the retained offline reference planner must choose the *same* method, direction, reverse length, terminal position and ordered guidance points. A false difference is a scheduler bug, not a new architectural decision. The shell test removes `coroutine` entirely, then compares complete plans at the original and alternate completion poses. Budget and incomplete-plan assertions prove no individual update can exceed the weighted geometry allowance; GIANTS must still validate real elapsed frame time.

**Non-changes:** no altered steering/pose semantics, no speed or brake-model modification, no extra behavioural interlocks or route choices, no change to Hold & Relocate or production completion transit, and no expansion beyond single-member Field 77 edge 9 experimental Control.
