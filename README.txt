v4.7.34 consolidates the already-established Traffic Policeman, Productive Continuation Preference, Encounter Maturation, Action-Space Compression, Preference-Band Exhaustion, Hold, Reposition, Representation Fitness and player-escalation architecture into one explicit Decision ordering contract.

Traffic Policeman is an omnipresent but normally dormant Decision responsibility. It becomes active only while current Reality requires decisive temporary movement ordering; once unrestricted cooperative movement is again supportable, its temporary `PROGRESS` / `YIELD` authority and purpose-bound restrictions expire even if the wider Encounter or Commitment still has restoration, handover or settlement work to complete.

The primary Traffic Policeman preference is strictly sequential in Decision: `CONTINUE_OBSERVATION → REGULATE_SPEED → HOLD_AT_SAFE_POINT → NATIVE_REPOSITION`. A later band may receive primary resolution authority only after all earlier bands are explicitly exhausted against the same current governing traffic requirement in the same Decision epoch. Candidates need not be physically attempted to prove exhaustion, and a material Reality/Control change starts a fresh epoch from the least-disruptive end.

`REGULATE_SPEED` is clarified as bounded GIANTS-owned progression: the worker may proceed or creep while GIANTS retains route, steering and forward/reverse choice. `HOLD_AT_SAFE_POINT` means the current realised occupancy itself is a supportable waiting place; movement to create a waiting place belongs to Reposition. `NATIVE_REPOSITION` is direction-agnostic and may include forward, reverse or composed bounded movement. Reposition exhaustion is participant-complete: both admissible Yield assignments and all positively supportable spatial candidates must be considered before autonomous resolution is declared unavailable.

Candidate `Purpose` remains an action-level result, not independent objective-setting authority. It must be traceable to current admitted intent/Operational Picture and, where a Commitment already exists, its Objective and unresolved Obligations. Candidate generators represent that Purpose; they do not invent the governing objective.

Production Decision remains passive, no live Commitment is applied and Control authority remains disabled. Reverse remains architecturally valid while OuttaMyWay-directed reverse actuation remains an implementation/evidence question.
