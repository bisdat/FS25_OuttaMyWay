# Blocked-First Situation Assessment — 0.5 Architecture

**Status:** accepted architectural direction and outstanding responsibilities under [issue #440](https://github.com/bisdat/FS25_OuttaMyWay/issues/440). This document records the accepted correction that **GIANTS `isBlocked` is the blockage authority**, rather than requiring OuttaMyWay to prove native blockage independently. The live TEST product now performs **server-gated Hold & Relocate Control** after independent Pair Commitment; passive native Observation itself still only reads GIANTS blocked-state fields, with no native event hook. **TEST 0.5.0.32 received a GIANTS in-game TS015 PASS verdict on 9 October 2026**, including reported successful handling of a late three-way blockage. This validates the observed TS015 run, not every field, implement configuration, or three-party situation.

## Purpose

**Reality qualification (TS003, TEST 0.5.1.1): FAIL.** Native Observation qualified a solo blocked worker for 1,008 ms, but strict root-in-polygon admission rejected it as `SINGLE_OUTSIDE_KNOWN_FIELD_POLYGONS` before TRANSIT or relocation. Root coordinates and boundary relationship were not recorded; field association versus polygon containment remains an architectural distinction requiring positive evidence. The field/margin contract has not been revised by this finding.

**TEST 0.5.1.2 / #461 correction:** Solo native blockage is expected **outside** the working field as well as inside it. GIANTS' current FIELDWORK Job Episode and sustained native blocked state suffice for the solo blocked-state boundary; the pairwise common-polygon check is not applicable. The 40 m oblique return region is defined from the worker's current root even when no registered field polygon contains it. A candidate endpoint inside a known field can be preferred, never required. **Solo has no 5 s blocker Regulation and no 7 s relocated-worker Hold**: successful region entry leads immediately to native FIELDWORK STOP/START. These explicit rules supersede the attempted solo field-membership assumption in TEST .1.1. Pairwise geometry, timers and admission remain unchanged.

**TEST 0.5.1.1 / #461 — single-worker extension:** A sustained native `isBlocked` episode may concern a single active field worker with no nearby eligible worker-pair candidate. No Local Worker Pair is not proof of no Native Blockage. After independent single-worker admission, the existing Hold & Relocate physical mechanisms execute **TRANSIT → oblique RELOCATE to a field-inward 40 m projected region → GIANTS FIELDWORK STOP → immediate START**. The pairwise Regulation of the other worker and paired relocated-worker 7-second Hold do not apply when there is no partner. This is a **TEST implementation hypothesis awaiting GIANTS Reality**, not an observed TS003 recovery success. The validated `0.5.1.0` paired behaviour remains unchanged.


OuttaMyWay exists to help a worker resume its GIANTS-owned autonomous activity when native blockage warrants intervention, with the **least disruptive justified response**. GIANTS decides that a worker is blocked. OMW does not duplicate native collision detection or reconstruct GIANTS' fieldwork route.

> **Native `isBlocked` = GIANTS' blocked-state evidence.**
>
> **Blocked-state evidence ≠ identity of blocking partner ≠ automatic authority to intervene.**

This is the architectural separation we need. Ordinary opposed A8 passing is expected to produce **less than one second of confirmed GIANTS blockage**, so the existing persistence gate filters it **without an A8 classifier**. GIANTS can also alternate blocked/unblocked while attempting to manoeuvre around a moving worker or static obstacle. These are authentic native states, not automatic intervention requests. The A8 duration is a working scenario assumption, not a universally demonstrated bound.

[Native Course Advancement](../docs/research/USEFUL_CONTINUATION_EVIDENCE.md) remains completed source research, **not** a required signal, dependency or gate. The physical-obstruction proof step proposed in the initial PR #446 draft is likewise **withdrawn**. **GIANTS retains native replanning authority** ([Project Vision](PROJECT_VISION.md)).

## Named evidence and responsibility concepts

| Concept | Architectural meaning | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Native Blockage Edge** | GIANTS reports `isBlocked=true` or `false` for an identifiable worker | Does not identify a causal partner |
| **Native Blockage Pulse** | An interval of positively observed native blocked=true, ending on its known false edge | Not necessarily a distinct obstruction |
| **Unresolved Obstruction Episode** | An evidence-bound blocked/retry concern within one active GIANTS Job Episode, possibly across several pulses associated with the same unresolved encounter | Must not merge unrelated blocked encounters merely because worker/job identity matches |
| **Native Blockage Persistence Gate** | At least **1.000 second of accumulated confirmed blocked time** in one unresolved episode, excluding periods positively observed unblocked | Admits *consideration* only, never automatic hold/relocation |
| **Spatial Pair Inference** | After the native one-second gate, nominate the **nearest other eligible worker within 30 m horizontal X/Z root-to-root distance** | This approximate position-based candidate need not report blocked and is not proven causal |
| **Situation Assessment** | Decide whether the native blocked condition and plausible worker relationship justify **considering a cooperative response**, or whether to leave GIANTS to recover / await evidence | Does **not** independently prove GIANTS' collision or blocked state |
| **Hold & Relocate** | After selecting one worker to relocate, temporarily hold nearby blocker(s) to protect egress, reverse the selected assembly towards the field centroid in TRANSIT, hold it to allow the blocker to resume, then stop and immediately restart its GIANTS AI job | Live server-side TEST mechanism now wired behind independent Pair Commitment; in-game effects not yet validated |

## 1. Trust GIANTS for the blocked state

GIANTS' `isBlocked` is the input authority. There is no need for OuttaMyWay to recreate the native collision system, calculate a contact overlap, classify hedge versus worker from a separate obstacle detector, or independently prove physical obstruction before accepting a native blocked assertion.

Native `false` means GIANTS ceased reporting blocked **at that instant**. It does not prove that a temporarily moving neighbour will not obstruct again. Conversely, native `true` is not proof that GIANTS' own manoeuvres will fail or that OMW should act immediately.

The preferred *candidate observation direction* is native **blocked/unblocked edge-driven evidence**, not indiscriminate 500 ms scanning of all workers. [TEST 0.5.0.5 research](../docs/research/NATIVE_BLOCKED_EVENT_TAP.md) observed both directions, including a 174 ms pulse, in FS25 1.24.0.0. The experimental global constructor hook was **retired** in TEST 0.5.0.6; it is **not** a verified public subscription and must not be restored merely because a signal contract has been described.

## 2. Filter transient native blockage with the accepted persistence gate

The accepted gate is **≥1 second of accumulated positively confirmed native blocked time** within one unresolved obstruction episode. **Below 1 second, OMW ignores the blockage for intervention purposes: no Spatial Pair Inference, Situation Assessment or resolution candidate.** A false edge closes a *pulse* and prevents time accruing while unblocked; it does **not necessarily** close the unresolved concern. Repeated true/false retries may contribute to the same concern **only while episode continuity is supportable**.

Do not accumulate blocked time across unrelated passing encounters, distinct obstacles at different places, new Job Episodes, missing/unknown observations or time imposed by OMW's later Control. Positive cessation of the encounter or Job Episode ends its eligibility. Targeted time evaluation may be needed after a true edge with no further notification, but does not imply a continuous all-worker scan.

**The gate is not a physical-obstruction proof step.** It already filters the expected short native assertions during ordinary opposed A8 passing; **no A8 recognition, classification, special distance, or dedicated exemption is required**. If ordinary successful passing later proves to generate ≥1 second of accumulated confirmed blockage, that challenges the duration assumption and should be recorded as Reality evidence—not pre-emptively patched with A8 logic. Reaching the gate allows generic Situation Assessment; it never grants movement authority.

## 3. Infer candidate worker pairs from positions

After a worker satisfies the gate, **choose the nearest other eligible local worker with a current assembly-root position within a 30-metre radius of the blocked worker's assembly root**, comparing horizontal **X/Z** coordinates. Deduplicate the root assemblies and exclude the blocked assembly itself. **The threshold is radius 30 m (distance ≤30 m), not diameter.** If there is no eligible worker in that radius, **there is no worker-pair candidate**; do not select a remote worker because it is the nearest available. **The candidate blocker need not report blocked.** This deliberately approximate root-position rule is accepted without implement geometry, clearance or route reconstruction.

`isBlocked` reports the **affected worker**, not the blocker. A nearby worker might be incidental to a hedge/tree blockage. That uncertainty belongs in **Situation Assessment and responsibility selection**, not in an additional physical-collision proof system.

**The 30 m radius is the accepted locality policy**, not an empirical physical-clearance or causal-contact threshold. No implement-width correction, vehicle-specific radius, collision hull, trajectory prediction or geometric precision is required. An in-radius worker is a *possible* blocker, not a proven one. If there is no candidate, do not invent a worker pair; where eligibility or attribution is uncertain, Situation Assessment may remain **WAITING_FOR_EVIDENCE**. Re-evaluate current positions/authority before any later Pair Commitment.

## Specification Jurisdiction — Spatial Pair Inference

**Jurisdiction ID:** `SPATIAL_PAIR_INFERENCE`  
**Primary Specification:** [`spec/SPATIAL_PAIR_INFERENCE.md`](../spec/SPATIAL_PAIR_INFERENCE.md)

Only the accepted **≥1 s confirmed blocked input and nearest eligible worker within 30 m** belong to this narrow evaluator contract. It does not capture GIANTS events, accumulate retry pulses, determine causal obstruction, assess the full Situation or authorize/control workers. Those responsibilities remain distinct.

## One physical worker pair, one candidate occurrence

Native blockage is worker-specific, so in an opposed encounter both workers may independently nominate one another after the same one-second gate. **Those are two observations of the same unordered worker pair, not two independent pair resolutions.** Observation unifies the active pair identity from the two assembly roots; it neither adds another blockage qualification gate nor selects movement roles.

The active occurrence retires when both workers are natively unblocked, either native job or strategy turns over, a participant disappears, the pair leaves the accepted 30 m neighbourhood, or OMW observation stops. A later qualifying encounter may nominate that pair again. This protects future coordinated Control from **competing reciprocal commitments**, without claiming that candidate observation itself grants Control.

## 4. Hold & Relocate — accepted six-stage architecture

**Architectural decision (9 October 2026):** This is the general operational architecture for resolving a worker-to-worker physical blockage. It is the target responsibility after the verified native blocked evidence and pair-candidate identification, **not a claim that current source already controls vehicles**.

1. **Native blockage:** react after GIANTS reports at least **1 second** of confirmed `isBlocked` evidence. Retain the existing one-second gate and the accepted nearest eligible candidate within **30 m** as the present upstream identification policy.
2. **Select a relocating worker:** of the two identified workers, choose the worker whose **current assembly root is nearest to the field centroid** (horizontal X/Z distance). This chooses the relocating participant, whether or not it was the one first reported natively blocked. A tie-breaking mechanism and acquisition of the correct field centroid remain implementation details; do not infer the centroid from the pair midpoint.
3. **Protect egress:** regulate the admitted other worker to **1 km/h for 5 seconds**, preserving native steering, acceleration, direction and drive permission. Release solely by timer; do not add a pairwise clearance gate. The wider blocker-neighbourhood question remains unresolved.
4. **Transit and relocation:** request TRANSIT and immediately use established GIANTS-native BWR reversing toward a **Cross-Track Egress Region** without awaiting fold readiness. The oblique vector length is **the blocker’s observed working width + 5 m**; 41 m is only the 36 m blocker example. Among polygon-admissible choices, prefer the direction **toward the field interior**, represented by the centroid-facing projection; polygon membership alone is not the preference. Reverse completes on signed cross-track region entry, not steering-point arrival, and has **no arbitrary movement-distance fail-safe**. The separate 30 m pair-candidate radius remains unchanged.
5. **Allow blocker continuation:** hold the relocated worker for **7 seconds** after region entry; release strictly by timer and hand back to native FIELDWORK without another pair-clearance gate. This is separate from the five-second other-worker Regulation.
6. **Native hand-back:** **stop the relocated worker's GIANTS AI job and immediately restart it**, returning route choice, productive configuration and continuation to GIANTS. OMW does not reconstruct a productive course or require an exact-axis return.

**Coordination and timed release:** one identified unordered pair is one temporary cooperative resolution. The five-second 1 km/h speed Regulation begins concurrently with TRANSIT request and reverse, and is released strictly by timer. After positive cross-track Region entry, the relocated worker is Held for seven seconds, then handed back to GIANTS by timer without a pair-clearance release gate. TRANSIT request is not physical fold readiness. Native FIELDWORK handback creates no historical job attempt record, whether its native calls succeed or fail. Each independently qualified collision starts from present GIANTS evidence. No automatic chord/travel-budget fail-safe is authorised; current physical overrides are released as far as immediately possible, with release failure reported without parking a commitment.

**GIANTS-native reverse method — established BWR implementation constraint:** The [GIANTS API surfaces](../docs/engine/GIANTS_API_SURFACES.md) and [GIANTS runtime knowledge](../docs/engine/GIANTS_RUNTIME_KNOWLEDGE.md) document the approved reverse control mechanics, previously realised by [archived `NativeDriveMechanism`](https://github.com/bisdat/FS25_OuttaMyWay/blob/archive/0.4.11.0/scripts/control/mechanisms/NativeDriveMechanism.lua) and [archived BWR Control](https://github.com/bisdat/FS25_OuttaMyWay/blob/archive/0.4.11.0/scripts/control/BlockedWorkerRecoveryControl.lua) (see [issue #336](https://github.com/bisdat/FS25_OuttaMyWay/issues/336)). Control MUST preserve:

- The reverse steering frame from `vehicle:getAIReverserNode()`, **not** the forward `getAISteeringNode()` frame for reverse target conversion.
- Where available, `AIVehicleUtil.getAIToolReverserDirectionNode(vehicle)` and the GIANTS-equivalent **tool-relative lateral/longitudinal target adjustment** for an articulated assembly; then transform the adjusted world target with `worldToLocal` against the vehicle reverser node.
- Native `AIVehicleUtil.driveToPoint()` semantics with `moveForwards=false` and its **local-space target coordinates**, preserving its optional `doNotSteer` input if interposing on an existing native call. A reverse flag alone does **not** reproduce native steering.
- BWR's separation of the reverse **steering look-ahead** from **Region completion**. The archived 40 m steering horizon supports articulated steering and is not an arrival point or a movement-distance abort.

This is a requirement to reuse the **verified reversing method**, not an instruction to restore the archived BWR architecture wholesale or to choose an interception mechanism before testing. The new centroid-directed target and bounded completion belong to Hold & Relocate, while native reverse geometry belongs to subordinate Control. The precise offset, steering target construction and interface composition still require implementation-facing specification and GIANTS Reality validation.

**TS015 geometric hypothesis — Width-Normalised Diagonal Egress (discussion, not yet accepted authority):** The project record proposes constructing a right-angle triangle from the relocating assembly's initial position: one conceptual leg of length `W = max(pair working widths)` along the **bearing toward the field centroid**, and a second leg of length `W` orthogonal to that bearing. The intended actual reverse command targets the **hypotenuse endpoint directly**, rather than first driving to a corner and then turning. For two 36 m working-width assemblies: `36 m + 36 m` orthogonal legs imply `sqrt(36^2+36^2)=50.9117 m` diagonal and a 45-degree bearing relative to the centroid line. The projection along the centroid bearing may pass the centroid; the centroid is a **directional preference, not a stopping point**. Name two separate concepts: **Egress Side** (choose the sign of the orthogonal component using the other worker's location/course, corridor and field boundary) and **Width-Normalised Egress Extent** (derive a desired displacement from pair working width, not from folded TRANSIT width). Working width is not a demonstrated minimum physical obstacle clearance. The nominal 50.91 m exceeds the live 30 m movement cap by ~20.91 m, so this proposal requires a separately approved bound and new GIANTS test; it is not an implicit offset grant. Moreover, a 45-degree endpoint command is not proof of a 45-degree physical reverse track, given finite steering/turning and an implement. Near the exact centroid, a centroid-bearing vector becomes undefined; it must never be normalised without evidence or replaced by a guessed heading. The field polygon, available edge space, swept clearance and **which** orthogonal side is safer must be evaluated before a proposed Control objective is authorised. Preserve the currently agreed 5 s/10 s timer-only Hold policy unless separately revisited. This paragraph records geometry for architectural evaluation and does **not** change today's executable contract.

**Discovery — Hold Command vs Hold Effect resolved narrowly:** In these TWO intervals, native interception plus near-zero root movement is affirmative evidence that the translation Hold **works while active**. The visual impression of an unheld Patriot should not be treated as a Hold-actuator failure without checking which five-second interval is being observed. The 5 s duration is still unvalidated as a sufficient **Protection Window**; at ideal steady 15 km/h it covers only ~20.83 m of actual diagonal motion (~14.73 m perpendicular at 45°), less after acceleration and steering. Expiry at 5 s occurs even if the relocating assembly has not reached physical clearance, because release is timer-only. Reconsidering the *duration* is a separate evidenced decision, not a retrofit of pairwise release gates.

**Disproven hypothesis — Width-Normalised Diagonal Endpoint implies safe settlement:** The 36-by-36 m construction determines a nominal **destination chord**, but does not prove a collision-free **swept Egress Corridor**, clearance relative to the other worker's next motion, or feasibility under articulated native reverse steering. Selecting the side that maximises root-to-root distance at the endpoint is an endpoint heuristic, **not a Clearance Point assessment**. GIANTS work width represents productive swath, not necessarily the assembly's folded physical envelope. TRANSIT requested concurrently with reverse does not establish when booms have reached a compact envelope. The recorded report of inconsistent steering and continued conflict is consistent with these missing concepts.

**Discovery — Chord Length vs Travel Budget:** The reverse mechanism integrates each actual root-position step into `travelledM`, yet the maximum distance is set to the straight-line hypotenuse `W√2`. Real turning/steering normally consumes extra path length; the destination can remain out of range when the bound is reached. This makes `REVERSE_BOUND_EXCEEDED` or `BOUND_REACHED_WITHOUT_TARGET` possible even when following a plausible physical route. Those causes are **hypotheses**, not established diagnoses of either recorded `FAILED_SAFE`, because the runtime publishes only the outcome `status` and drops the preserved `reason` at the publication boundary (**Failure-Reason Erasure**).

**Discovery — Fixed Steering Horizon is not a route follower:** `NativeReverseMechanism:startReverse` fixes a 40 m steering-reference point in world space at reverse start, while the authorised diagonal endpoint is 50.91 m away. Passing the 40 m reference would move that fixed target behind the machine, a credible source of steering instability; no GIANTS path/heading trace was captured to prove that this happened. The two values must not be conflated, and merely increasing the numeric steering horizon is not an architecture for feasible reverse navigation.

**Discovery — Post-Failure Recommitment:** `FAILED_SAFE` closes the current commitment, after which a new one-second blocked occurrence can immediately create another commitment for the SAME unordered pair, now with the other selected relocator and opposing egress side. TS015 visibly exercised this sequence approximately **1.126 s** after first failure. This can produce alternating unresolved manoeuvres without demonstrating that conditions have improved. A **Post-Failure Disposition** concept is missing: distinguish safely neutralised physical effects from permission to reattempt the unchanged physical encounter; this is not a new pairwise Hold release gate.

**Architecture status and next Observe→Discuss→Hypothesise loop:** Hold physical suppression during the measured 5 s is increasingly supported. The .28 direct-endpoint geometry, its `W√2` travel budget, inferred Egress Side, and five-second protection sufficiency are **not field-validated**; do not increase width, steering distance or timer blindly. Before another manoeuvre change, retain/publish reverse failure `reason`, `travelledM`, remaining distance to target, steering reference relative to pose, start/end root headings, and configuration-state evidence, together with the physical clearance status of both assemblies. Evaluate **Egress Corridor**, **Clearance Point**, **Protection Window**, **Feasible Reverse Path**, and **Post-Failure Disposition** as distinct architectural concepts. Keep the current no-pairwise-release-gate policy until an explicit contrary decision; treat field reality, not offline mocks, as the authority.

### Architectural correction following TS015 .28 — 9 October 2026

### TS015 TEST 0.5.0.29 — Axial Retreat Is Not Cross-Track Egress (2026-10-09)

**OBSERVATION:** The uploaded `0.5.0.29_TS015.zip` GIANTS log shows a native blocked candidate at **14:24:30.319**, root pair `396362|400521`, 1,004 ms persisted, **9.5978 m** root distance. Video around 14:24:30–14:24:42 corroborates the two assembly centres remaining almost aligned in the filmed view; no reliable precise world-frame lateral-distance measurement was captured.

**DISCOVERY — Axial Retreat Trap:** `ProjectedEgressRegion.plan` considers `AWAY_FROM_OTHER` a candidate direction. When two workers face each other along essentially the same line, this vector is primarily **longitudinal**. `ProjectedEgressRegion.progress` then tests only positive projection onto the chosen reverse direction. Therefore a 20 m longitudinal retreat can satisfy the .29 stopping criterion while the relocated assembly still occupies the other worker's intended longitudinal travel corridor. This is a **policy/geometry defect**, not demonstrated failure of GIANTS native reversing or missing Regulation. The archive's BWR **Return Region** was faithfully adapted as a *retreat* concept; it was wrongly interpreted as sufficient for *encounter egress*. The recorded requirement in a head-on encounter is **lateral separation**.

**ARCHITECTURAL CONSEQUENCE — Cross-Track Egress Region:** Replace the retreat-only settlement rule with an inexpensive **lateral-separation condition** in the local coordinate frame of a supported blocker travel axis. Let `t` be the blocker's supported native course axis, `n=(-t_z,t_x)` its unit normal, and `d=P_relocator-P_blocker` the relative horizontal position. Then `longitudinal=d·t` and `crossTrack=d·n` are two constant-time projections. An egress region must demand the correctly signed cross-track separation beyond a **defensible physical corridor bound**; large longitudinal retreat alone never satisfies it in a head-on alignment. The bound must derive from evidenced relevant **TRANSIT physical envelopes + nominal clearance**, not the rejected 36 m productive work swath, unmeasured metadata or a new hard-coded guarantee. The archived physical-occupancy study remains a limit on that evidence, not permission to guess. If only projected lateral progress is supportable, name it **Lateral Progress Region** and explicitly withhold physical-clearance claims.

**STEERING vs COMPLETION:** A lateral completion predicate alone cannot fix a reverse steering command directed straight away from the other worker. A practical selected **oblique rearward vector**, admissible for native articulated reverse and field boundaries, must have a **positive cross-track component** toward a chosen Egress Side. Choosing the side is a local, bounded decision using relative worker positions, native course headings and available field space, not a return to the W×W/45° triangle. Monitor realised displacement/projection and native reverse heading separately from the world steering reference. Do not make exact 45° steering, a fixed future waypoint or target-radius arrival into the desired physical result.

**LOW COMPUTATIONAL COST:** Two dot products for lateral/longitudinal progress per update are O(1). Two candidate side directions with once-per-commitment field-segment sampling remain bounded and cheap. No continuous collision planner, fieldwide sweep or pairwise release gate is required to discover/measure the lateral deficit. The five-second 1 km/h Regulation and ten-second relocated-worker Hold remain unchanged until distinct evidence justifies changing their timers. Do not reintroduce the disallowed `FAILED_SAFE` or arbitrary chord-distance abort.

**STATUS — observe/discuss before implementation:** TEST .29 is **not a successful lateral-egress implementation** despite a logged accepted GIANTS restart; the low-cost Cross-Track Egress contract is now the design candidate, *not yet approved executable source*. Next validate current course-axis availability, representable TRANSIT envelope/clearance threshold, safe lateral side, and desired vs actual initial reverse bearing. Update the Specification and tests before a subsequent TEST build. ## 5. Keep Situation Assessment and Hold & Relocate responsibility distinct

**Situation Assessment** examines native blockage persistence, present candidate pair proximity and whether a paired response is justified. It can decline intervention when GIANTS is evidently recovering or the blocking pair is unsupported, but **does not require an independently measured physical overlap or GIANTS course advancement**.

Only a downstream, explicitly authorised **Hold & Relocate responsibility** may operate the accepted six-stage sequence. **Relocation selection is centroid-nearest; other-worker Regulation lasts 5 s and the relocated-worker Hold lasts 7 s, independently timer-released, with no pairwise release gates; GIANTS-native BWR reversing semantics are mandatory.** The means of acquiring the field centroid, the physical mechanisms, handling additional nearby blockers, bounded movement completion, lifecycle invalidation and the exact offset remain unresolved at implementation-facing detail. A held worker's resulting inactivity must not be counted as fresh evidence of its native inability to progress. GIANTS retains its own productive route and replanning.

## Architectural invariants

- **Native `isBlocked` is authoritative blocked-state evidence**; do not build another blocked-state detector or independently require physical contact proof.
- **1 second confirmed blocked time** is a consideration gate; known unblocked time contributes zero.
- Separate pulses can belong to one unresolved concern; a false edge does not alone mean durable recovery.
- The worker that GIANTS reports blocked is known; **the identity of its blocker is not**.
- After the ≥1 s gate, **the nearest eligible worker at current horizontal assembly-root distance ≤30 m** is the candidate; a worker beyond the radius is not. Simultaneous blocked flags are neither necessary nor sufficient for causal identification.
- Native blockage below the one-second gate produces **no OMW intervention candidate**, regardless of the encounter type; no A8-specific filter or classifier exists.
- The **30 m root-position radius is only for candidate selection**, not an independent physical-clearance or blockage proof. Implement geometry, course progression, agronomic output and trajectory reconstruction are not required.
- Worker/Job Episode changes, player intervention and OMW-imposed control effects invalidate or quarantine prior evidence as appropriate.
- Missing or contradictory evidence remains **WAITING_FOR_EVIDENCE**, not permission to act.
