# Prototype 31 — Purpose-Bound Progression Preservation Shadow Probe

## Purpose

Test whether the canonical Regulation model can be represented generally as **bounded consumption of positively supported native progression while preserving purpose-bound Action Space**, without relying on leader/follower, surprise-head-on, or exact-route special cases.

The probe is intentionally passive. It does not choose a speed, Hold, Reposition, Refuge role, route, or Control action.

## Inputs promoted into the sealed Operational Picture

- realised motion evidence: reported and displacement-derived speed, actual travel direction, heading/travel relationship, motion classification, Local Intent classification/epoch/validity, and Job Episode token;
- configuration-filtered positive Physical Assembly primitives used only for positive represented-conflict support;
- existing Current Space, Future Space, Committed Demand, Potential Demand, Temporary Slack, Representation Fitness and uncertainty;
- immutable meaning of currently open Commitment obligations, not merely their IDs.

## Shadow progression test

For a participant with positive settled Local Intent and a realised travel direction consistent with the currently represented field-bounded continuation:

1. project each known positive Physical Assembly primitive along the realised native travel vector, bounded by the current Local Intent horizon;
2. test for positive represented intersection with:
   - another participant's represented Current Space;
   - representable field-bounded Committed/Potential Demand;
   - a coarse uncontaminated demonstrated boundary-maturation demand witness supplied by the existing headland evidence probe;
3. record the first **known represented witness entry distance** within that bounded continuation;
4. preserve the initial witness distance as the stable baseline for the same subject intent epoch and witness evidence basis;
5. report how much of that known headroom has subsequently been consumed;
6. invalidate rather than extrapolate when the Local Intent epoch or witness evidence basis changes.

The boundary-maturation adapter is a test witness, not a new Space class or production demand authority.

## Authority limits

`knownWitnessEntryM` is **not safe clearance**. Current representation permits positive conflict support but does not generally permit negative-clearance conclusions. Unrepresented geometry may invalidate space earlier than the known witness.

The probe therefore reports:

- `negativeClearanceAuthority=false`;
- `responseOpportunity=UNMODELED`;
- `responseAdjustedSupportableProgression=UNRESOLVED`;
- `decisionAuthority=false`;
- `speedAuthority=false`;
- `controlAuthority=false`.

No reaction-time, stopping-distance, speed, distance, or braking literal gains architectural authority.

## Expected evidence in the Condor / Patriot scenario

During the trailing phase, Patriot should obtain a stable `MATURATION_WITNESS` against Condor's demonstrated boundary demand. As Patriot advances, `consumedFromBaseline` should increase even if the existing D-0126 speed cap rises. That will directly expose whether the active test Regulation is spending the same Maturation margin it previously created.

When Condor enters TURNING/opposed continuation, the old maturation witness should invalidate with the old intent basis. Current/Committed-Demand witnesses may then become the relevant positive geometry. The probe must not manufacture a new route or a speed recommendation.

## Sliding-puzzle criterion

The same shadow geometry must remain meaningful when a worker is not a follower and is not heading toward another worker's current footprint, but its native progression would consume represented demand or action opportunity needed elsewhere. If the representation requires separate follower/head-on/sliding-puzzle branches, the generalisation hypothesis is weakened and architecture should be revisited before production Regulation work.
