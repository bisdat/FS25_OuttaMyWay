2. **Observation and identity** — raw Observation Snapshot, stable assembly identity and Job Episode evidence/admission. **v4.7.1 canonical**
3. **Knowledge boundary** — admitted Operation identity, Situation Assessment, immutable Operational Picture, uncertainty and Representation Fitness. **v4.7.2 canonical**
4. **Deterministic Decision boundary** — complete supportable Candidate Action Space, explicit mandatory Constraint Verdicts and deterministic Decision Records. **v4.7.3 candidate**
5. **Replay conformance** — historical positive and negative fixtures; no physical authority.
6. **Passive live validation** — replacement traces only, selected legacy authority absent, zero Control.
7. **First exclusive vertical slice** — one bounded Hold/release lifecycle owned entirely by the replacement core.
8. **Controlled expansion** — multi-stage refuge, lifecycle boundaries and broader fixtures one canonical responsibility at a time.

## Build-economy rule

Pure contracts and deterministic reasoning are disproved offline before a game package is requested. Live build/test cycles are reserved for propositions requiring GIANTS Reality.

## Standing prohibitions

- no inference of architecture from archived code;
- no parallel legacy Decision core;
- no active import from `scripts/archive/`;
- no architectural additions during implementation;
- no Control authority before replay and passive gates.
