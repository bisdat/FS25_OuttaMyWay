# Architectural Seminar Series

> **Authority:** Durable discovery record
>
> **Currency:** Last reviewed for canonical release v4.5.8
>
> **Lifecycle:** Active research record

This series records how the architecture evolved from Seminar 01 onward. It preserves observations, hypotheses, decreasing confidence and rejected ideas rather than presenting every discussion as an accepted decision.

## Seminar 01 — What is a Conflict Zone?

**Initial hypothesis:** Conflict Zone might be a root architectural concept: a spatial-temporal region where intended motion cannot safely coexist.

**Discovery:** repeated examination reduced confidence that Conflict Zone is primitive. It increasingly appears to be a consequence of more fundamental spaces, relationships, risk and available action. Conflict Zone remains operationally useful, but its root architectural status is under review.

## Seminar 02 — Future Space

**Discovery:** intelligent behaviour does not predict one future. It maintains a set of plausible futures currently under consideration.

**Working definition:** Future Space is the set of plausible future situations currently under consideration.

**Consequence:** risk is evaluated across plausible futures rather than attached to one prediction.

## Seminar 03 — Action Space

**Discovery:** expert behaviour preserves available actions rather than merely improving recovery after options collapse.

**Working definition:** Action Space is the set of actions currently available.

**Candidate principle:** the purpose of anticipation is not to improve recovery; it is to reduce the need for recovery.

## Seminar 04 — Situation Space

Situation Space survived repeated attempts to replace or subdivide it. The proposed `Conditions` concept was rejected because weather and comparable influences already belong within Situation Space.

The understanding evolved from “everything relevant now” toward a structural interpretation: Situation Space describes the connected topology of reality relevant to navigation, including entities, relationships and states.

A further distinction emerged:

- **Situation Space** — the set of possible situations and relationships.
- **Current Situation** — one point within that space.

This distinction remains an active architectural hypothesis rather than an accepted replacement for existing terminology.

## Seminar 05 — Reality and Knowledge

Reality and the system's knowledge of reality are not identical.

```text
Reality evolves
    ↓
Observations are sampled
    ↓
Situation Assessment updates Knowledge
```

The system possesses an estimate, not reality itself. This distinction explains uncertainty, hidden hazards, incomplete observation and delayed understanding without requiring special cases.

The repository term `Operational Picture` may describe the same thing as the emerging `Current Situation`. That possible duplication is deferred for explicit review.

`Entity` is the current provisional replacement candidate for `Actor`; confidence in the concept is high, while confidence in the label remains low.

## Seminar 06 — Time

Every earlier diagram was a snapshot. Time is not merely another object in the architecture; it is the dimension in which Reality evolves, observations occur, Knowledge changes, Future Space is reconsidered and Action Space expands or collapses.

Elite racing drivers' reports that time appears slower suggest a useful interpretation: expertise may spend less effort reconstructing the present and more of the available interval exploring plausible futures.

**Candidate maxim:** expertise is not primarily thinking faster; it is spending less time discovering where you already are.

## Current Research Position

The seminar series increases confidence in a family of Spaces and Transformations while deliberately withholding final promotion of several terms. Architecture must continue to be discovered through evidence rather than completed by vocabulary alone.
